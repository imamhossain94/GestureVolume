# Store art and videos

Everything here is drawn from the app's own code (its Compose screens, AGSL shaders, vector
drawables and strings) by the renderer in [`tool/store`](../tool/store), not captured on a phone.
Compare a few screens with a real device before you publish.

## Google Play

| File | Size | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| `graphics/feature-graphic.png` | 1024 × 500 | Main store listing › Feature graphic. Option A, from the brief in `play-listing-en.md`. |
| `graphics/extras/feature-graphic-b.png` | 1024 × 500 | The alternative: the icon and name beside a phone with the Deck open. |
| `graphics/screenshots/bleed/01–08` | 1080 × 1920 | Phone screenshots, in this order. Light, with the phone large and cropped. |
| `graphics/screenshots/card/01–08` | 1080 × 1920 | The same eight, dark, with the whole phone. Use one style or the other. |
| `graphics/extras/*/09-coin.png` | 1080 × 1920 | The ninth shot from the brief. Play takes eight phone screenshots, so it is a spare. |

The screenshots are 24-bit PNG with no alpha, as Play requires. Their captions:

| # | Title | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Volume on the edge | Swipe the bar and the Quick slider follows your finger |
| 02 | Pull out the Deck | Your apps, people and tools, one swipe in from the bar |
| 03 | Turns with your phone | On its side, the bar lies along the top edge |
| 04 | Fills that move | Live shaders, your colours and a flourish at 100% |
| 05 | Answers as you type | Find apps and people, or work out a sum, right in the Deck |
| 06 | Make it yours | Presets, size, shape, colour and icon, with a live preview |
| 07 | Hold for more | A long-press menu of your own, in glass or solid |
| 08 | Out of the way | Hide the bar in the apps you choose, or while you type |
| 09 | Heads or tails | A coin, dice, a timer and notes, all in the Deck |

Play's promo video is a YouTube link: upload `video/promo-1080p.mp4`, then paste its URL into Main
store listing › Video. Play then lays a play button over the middle of the feature graphic, which
both options keep clear.

## YouTube

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `video/promo-1080p.mp4` | The promo, 0:30, 1920 × 1080, cut to its music. |
| `video/promo-vertical.mp4` | The same, 1080 × 1920, for Shorts. |
| `video/promo-thumbnail.png` | Its thumbnail, 1280 × 720. |
| `tutorials/NN-name.mp4` | One tutorial per feature, 1920 × 1080, in series order. |
| `tutorials/NN-name.srt` | English subtitles for each, the same words as the on-screen captions. |
| `tutorials/NN-name.json` | Each tutorial's steps, chapters and subtitle timings. |
| `tutorials/thumbnails/NN-name.png` | Thumbnails, 1280 × 720. |
| `tutorials/youtube.md` | Titles, descriptions with chapters, tags, and a voice-over script for each video. |

The videos have music and sound effects, mixed to about −14 LUFS, and no voice. The music is
generated by `tool/store/audio.py`; the tick on each volume step is the app's own
`res/raw/slider_tick.wav`.

## Making it again

See the commands at the top of each script in [`tool/store`](../tool/store): `render-graphics.mjs`
and `finish.py` for Play, `render.mjs` for the videos, `render-thumbs.mjs`, `youtube.mjs` and
`gallery.mjs` for the rest. Shaders, drawables and strings are read from the app, so after a change
to the app, a re-run picks it up.

To check one screen against the app after a change, render its test page on its own, for example
`node render.mjs still test_fills check.png` for every Quick slider fill; the pages are
`tool/store/scenes/test_*.html`, and `tool/store/dev` has the debugging helpers.
